## Implementing CKB Vote Cells in ContestLedger

### My Aim 
My target this week was to change ContestLedger's signature-based voting to real CKB transactions. So any vote made by user becomes a Vote Cell on CKB testnet and is permanently recorded, publicly auditable, and independently verifiable without the database. This is the final piece need to make my project's entire data model trustless.
