### What I Studied
- What Spore Protocol is and how it works on CKB
- Differences between Spores and traditional Ethereum NFTs
- What Clusters are and how Clusters group spores into collections
- Best way to mint a Spore DOB on CKB testnet

### What I Learned
- What a spore is: it's a CKB version of an NFT, but different in one critical way, all content lives on-chain inside the cell data field, no external server needed, unlike the regular NFTs on Ethereum that allow tokens to live on-chain but content (images/metadata) lives on IPFS or a server
- The advantage of the Spore compared to the regular Ethereum NFT is that you actually own the content itself, unlike the regular Ethereum NFT, which you just own a receipt pointing to something, not the thing
- The benefits of using Spore in my project are that the winners' certificates will be permanent. I cannot delete them. They belong to the winner forever.
- learnt about the three Spore operations:
    - MINT - this is just the creation process of a new Spore 
    - TRANSFER - Sending the created Spore to another address
    - MELT - This process sees the Spores destroyed, and the CKB capacity is recovered
- I learnt about Clusters: a cluster is a collection of spores. Like for my project, a cluster can be created called "ContestLedger Winners" and all the winner certificates minted into that cluster. This makes the winners' certificates verifiably official, that they belong to the ConsteLedger collection, not any random Spores from the internet.
