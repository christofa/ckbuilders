### What I learned 
- What a spore is: it's a CKB verision of an NFT but different in one crirtical way, all content lives on-chain inside the cell data field, no external server needed unlike the regular NFTs on Ethereum that allows tokens live on-chain but content (images/metadata) lives on IPFS or a server
- The advantage of the Spore compared to the regular Ethereum NFT is that you actually own the content itself unlike thr regular Ethereum NFT that you just own a recipet pointing to something, not the thing
- The benefits of using Spore in my project is that winners certificates won will be permanent. I cannot delete them. They belong to the winner forever.
- learnt about the three Spore operations:
    - MINT - this is just the creating process of a new Spore 
    - TRANSFER - Sending the created Spore to another address
    - MELT - This process sees the Spores destroyed and the CKB capacity is recovered
- I learnt about Clusters: a cluster is a collection name for Spores. Like for my project, a cluster can be created called "ContestLedger Winners" and all winner certificates mint into that cluster. This make the winners certificates verifiably offical that they belong to ConsteLedger collection, not any random Spores from the internet.
