As the minimum reward equals 100 CKB, which is equal to 10,000,000,000 shannons, the threshold is safe enough for separation of old and new data.

- The Display vs The Storage

Using the single `shannonsToCkb()` function allows us to keep the logic of conversion of values in one place.

- Outpoints make relationships native to the chain
A foreign key in a database can guarantee the existence of the relationship only inside the database itself.
However, an on-chain outpoint ensures that the contest-entry relationship is verifiable on CKB.
