### About ContestLeger and it's importance
The online contest process is flawed. Brands conduct contests
using social media such as Instagram and Tiktok, where the
participants are actually working, and yet the winners are chosen
by an opaque algorithm that no one can check. ContestLedger
offers a solution by taking the entire contest life cycle on
the CKB blockchain. The rewards are pre-committed to prior to the
contest, votes are conducted using the blockchain technology,
winners selected through tallying of the votes, and nothing can
be altered – not even by myself or the brand itself.

So this creates the following for users:
- Awareness of ongoing contest opportunities
- Transparency betwween brands and participants in the ongoing contests
- Good interactions and publicity for brands
- Trust to users as results cannot be changed

### Project simple Flow
- Contest rewards locked in a CKB Cell (escrow) before launch
- Entries stored with IPFS content hash on-chain
- Votes are verifiable on-chain transactions
- Winners automatically receive xUDT token rewards
- Winners get a Spore DOB certificate as permanent proof of winning

### Tech Stacks I will be using
- Next.js + tailwind + Schadcn -> for the frontend designs
- @ckb-cc/core  -> for CKB transactions 
- xUDT  -> contest reward tokens
- spore DOBs    -> Winner certificates 
- IPFS  -> Content storage 
- Fiber Network -> for voting micropayments

Other tools maybe added as the projects goes on, but this are the main tools for now
