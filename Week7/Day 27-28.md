## The Pages and their Functions

### The Landing Page
![Screenshot 15](../screenshots/Screenshot%202026-05-14%20054212.png)

- The hero communicates the value immediately: "Create Contests. Submit Entries. Win Fairly. On-chain."

Three parts:
- Hero with calls to action (Create a Contest / Browse Contests)
- "How it works": Three steps: Create -> Enter -> Win
- Trust indicators: "Rewards escrowed", "Votes on blockchain",
  "Winners verified for eternity."

The trust indicators were the critical design choice.
Not many people trust online contests. The first thing that most users
see is the guarantee that their money will be escrowed before the contest starts, which removes that doubt immediately.

### Dashboard
![Screenshot 15](../screenshots/Screenshot%202026-05-14%20054251.png)

Contests have:
- The title and description of the contest
- Amount of tokens awarded for winning
- Countdown timer to the live deadline
- Number of entries and number of votes
- Status icon (Active, Ending soon, Closed)

A “Trending” section was added at the top for the
contests with the highest votes. This replicates the
viral content strategy used in TikTok and Instagram.

### Create Contest Page
![Screenshot 15](../screenshots/Screenshot%202026-05-14%20054333.png)

Input forms:
- Title/description
- Entry type selector (Image/Video/Text)
- Deadline date picker
- Reward amount field
- Treasury lock display icon – padlock icon appears,
  with the message, “Your reward will be locked
  on-chain before your contest launches. Once you’ve
  launched your contest, you can’t withdraw this
  reward.”

I deliberately designed the user interface for the
treasury lock. As a creator, I want you to see this badge
before you even launch a contest.

### components built and their function

- ContestCard: shows the countdown timer, status badge, and reward amount
- Navbar: shows connected wallet addresses and links users to the login/signup page
- TrustBadge: Used on the contest cards, landing page, and detail page

### What I learnt
The creation of the Create Contest form forced me to consider
the user experience flow even further. The brand that’s creating
a contest must be sure before they spend their tokens.
Each step of the form should make them feel this way,
which is why I put the badge on the lock for the treasury
so prominently.
