# Day 45-48: June 22nd - June 26th, 2026

## Fiber Network Deep Dive Before Hackathon 

### What I studied
As the ContestLedger's MVP was submitted, this week was spent on gaining a  proper understanding of the Fiber Network, which is the CKB technology that I had decided to use for the voting feature initially but postponed till now. Instead of just going through some documentation, I created models on CCC Playground in order to convince myself about the importance of Fiber.

### What I learned
- What Fiber Network actually is
- The three stages of a payment channel
    - The Open Stage
    - The Update Stage
    - The Close Stage
    - The Result Stage
- Why this matters specifically for ContestLedger
    - How it makes voting on ContestLedger very easy and smooth
    - More importantly, how it helps controls the gas fees
- How Fiber differs from Bitcoin Lightning Network
- What implementing real Fiber voting would require
